How the calculation works
Shot geometry is calculated from the actual table coordinate system, ball diameter, pocket openings, cushion centerlines, object-ball travel path, and cue-ball contact point.

Direct shots use ghost-ball collision geometry. Banks use reflected-pocket geometry and an actual cushion intersection. Obstructions and combination routes are checked before a route is accepted.

Cue-ball instructions use normal pool terminology such as center ball, follow, draw, left/right English, inside English, outside English, stun, and speed control. These describe the intended cue-ball action rather than inventing directional language.

Real tables introduce additional variables including speed, spin, throw, squirt, swerve, cloth, cushion response, pocket shelf depth, and table condition.
