How the calculation works
Shot Caller uses one physical coordinate system for the table, ball positions and shot calculations. Ball diameter, cushion centerlines and pocket openings are accounted for when evaluating direct, cut, bank and combination routes.

A route is rejected when the required ball path, cue-ball path, cushion contact or intermediate-ball clearance is physically blocked. The displayed route is the same route used by the solver.

Real tables introduce additional variables including speed, spin, throw, cloth, cushion response, pocket shelf depth and table condition.
