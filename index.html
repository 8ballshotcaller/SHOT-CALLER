Geometry uses a regulation 9-foot table model with a 2.25-inch ball. One-rail banks are solved using reflected target geometry and validated against ball-center clearance, cushion access, pocket-mouth clearance, and cue-ball travel. Real tables can vary slightly in pocket facing and cushion response, so the model deliberately favors practical shots rather than claiming laboratory-level prediction.
