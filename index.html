How It Works

Drag the cue ball and object balls directly on the table. The planner evaluates the selected pocket, collision geometry, table boundaries, blockers, and available routes.

For a bank or kick, the rail reflection is calculated before the recommendation is shown.

The red X beside a ball deletes that ball. It is deliberately separated from the ball's drag area.
