How the calculation works
Shot Caller uses one physical coordinate system for the table, balls, cushions and shot routes. Direct shots are solved from ghost-ball collision geometry. Banks use reflected-pocket geometry against the ball-center cushion line. Other balls in play are treated as physical obstructions, so a route is not shown when another ball would actually block it.

Combination routes are also evaluated when a second object ball can legally redirect the first ball toward the pocket.

Real tables introduce additional variables including speed, spin, throw, cloth, cushion response, pocket shelf depth and table condition.
