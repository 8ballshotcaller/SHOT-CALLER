Shot Caller uses a two-dimensional geometric model of a seven-foot table and regulation ball diameter. Direct shots use ghost-ball geometry. One-rail banks use reflected-pocket geometry and are validated against rail contact, pocket approach, cue-ball reachability, and obstruction. This model does not claim to simulate spin, throw, cloth speed, cushion compression, or table-specific pocket facings.
