How the calculation works
Shot Caller uses one physical coordinate system for the table graphics and shot solver. A standard 2.25-inch pool ball is used. Cut shots use ghost-ball collision geometry. One-rail banks use reflected-pocket geometry against the cushion centerline. The optional 8-ball is treated as a real physical obstruction and is checked against cue-ball, object-ball, and bank routes. The route shown on the table is the same route used by the calculation.

Real tables introduce additional variables such as speed, spin, throw, cloth, cushion response, pocket shelf depth, and table condition.
