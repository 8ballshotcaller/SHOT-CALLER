Shot Caller

Ball Placement

Target Ball: The ball you are trying to pocket.
Blocking Ball: A ball that can interfere with the cue-ball or object-ball path.
You can place multiple blocking balls, including both your balls and your opponent's balls.
Select a ball, choose its role, then tap the table to place it.
